# Your Six P's for Your Bookkeeping Service

Here's a framework tailored to your e-commerce bookkeeping niche:

## 1. **People** 
E-commerce founders/owners with $1-5M revenue doing everything themselves (or poorly delegated). Busy, growth-focused, not accountant-minded.

## 2. **Problem**
- Don't know which products/channels are actually profitable
- Tax deadlines terrify them (compliance blindness)
- Wasting 10+ hours/month on scattered accounting
- Making pricing decisions without real cost data
- Worried about IRS audits and financial chaos

## 3. **Promise**
Clean books + actionable profit insights delivered monthly—so you know exactly what's working, never miss a tax deadline, and make decisions with confidence.

## 4. **Proof**
- Client testimonials ("I found out 40% of my revenue was unprofitable")
- Before/after financial dashboards
- Case studies showing clients who recovered 5+ hours/month
- Your background/credentials

## 5. **Product**
Monthly bookkeeping (reconciliation, P&L by channel/SKU), tax coordination prep, dashboard reporting, basic financial advisory

## 6. **Price/Positioning**
$3,000/month = way cheaper than hiring someone full-time ($50-80k/year), way better than DIY mistakes, way simpler than big accounting firms (who charge $7-10k+)
