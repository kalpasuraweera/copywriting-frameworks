# Your Six P's

| P | Your Offer |
|---|-----------|
| **People** | E-commerce brand owners/operators, $1–5M/year revenue, likely on Shopify or multi-channel. Core pain: no clarity on profit by product/channel, time bleeding into spreadsheets or avoidance, tax surprises, can't optimize ad spend because they don't know real margins. |
| **Positioning** | "Bookkeeping built for e-commerce, not inherited from a dentist's accountant." You understand inventory swings, multi-channel complexity, cash-flow timing (seasonal, refunds), COGS. Generic bookkeepers treat you like a service business and miss everything that matters. |
| **Promise** | **Outcome:** clarity on what's actually profitable + decision-making power on ad spend + 10+ hours/month back. **Feature** would be "monthly bookkeeping"; **outcome** is "you know which 2–3 products aren't pulling their weight and can act on it." |
| **Proof** | Proof lives in your positioning (you speak the language of their business). Strongest proof: real client results ("our brands typically discover $X in margin leaks in their top 3 products," or "we've helped 50+ brands at your stage"). Without specific metrics, lean on the process—the fact that you set them up *right* is itself credible. |
| **Priority** | Cost of inaction (strongest): every month flying blind = overspending on bad channels, missing margin upside, seasonal cash crunches that blindside you. Tax time is chaotic. |
| **Process** | Pull from Shopify + ad accounts + payment processors → set books up for e-commerce (not generic) → deliver dashboard showing profit by product/channel + [monthly reporting / quarterly calls]. This is different from "we file your return." |

## Notes

**Key assumption:** audience is the owner/decision-maker, not a controller or accountant (copy is direct, assumes you have authority to hire). If you're selling to CFOs instead, we'd shift tone and add credential.

**To customize:** if you have a specific client result (e.g., "our clients discover 2–3 unprofitable products on average," or "brands save $X annually in caught expenses"), add that after "where the leaks are"—it's your strongest proof point and will lift conversions.
