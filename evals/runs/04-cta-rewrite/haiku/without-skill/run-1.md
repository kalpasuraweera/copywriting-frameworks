# Your Current CTA Problem
Your CTA is **too passive and vague**. It:
- Asks permission rather than commands action
- Offers no urgency or benefit
- Uses weak language ("feel free")
- Doesn't specify what happens next

---

## High-Converting Alternatives

**Option 1: Direct + Benefit-Driven**
> "Get a free [specific offer] — book your [call/demo/consultation] now"

**Option 2: Urgency + FOMO**
> "See how [specific outcome] — schedule your [timeframe: 15-min] discovery call today"

**Option 3: Curiosity + Action**
> "Discover if [your solution] fits your needs — [button: Start Here]"

**Option 4: Specificity + Ease**
> "[Send a message below / Click here] and I'll respond within 24 hours"

**Option 5: Scarcity + Benefit**
> "Claim your free [audit/strategy session] — only [X spots/this week]"

---

## Why These Work Better
✅ **Action verbs** (Get, Start, Claim, Discover)  
✅ **Specific benefit** (what they actually get)  
✅ **Urgency/scarcity** (time or availability)  
✅ **Clear next step** (what to click/do)  
✅ **Removes friction** (easy to understand)
