# 5 Twitter Thread Hooks for a Meal-Kit Brand (Busy Parents)

**Hook 1: Problem-Relatable**
"5:30 PM hits and you're still asking 'what's for dinner?' Meanwhile your kids are hungry, you're exhausted, and takeout again feels like defeat. What if dinner was already solved?"

**Hook 2: Surprising Statistic**
"Busy parents spend an avg of 8+ hours a week on meal planning & grocery shopping. That's 416 hours a year you're NOT with your family. Here's how we reclaim that time 👇"

**Hook 3: Bold Question**
"Real talk: Why are you still pretending you enjoy meal planning when you could spend those hours literally anywhere else? A thread on redefining dinner ⬇️"

**Hook 4: Curiosity/Intrigue**
"We asked 500 parents 'what would make dinnertime less stressful?' Their #1 answer surprised us (hint: it's not what you'd expect) 🧵"

**Hook 5: Emotional/Aspirational**
"Imagine: home-cooked meals on the table, happy kids actually eating it, and YOU not stressed or covered in flour. It's possible. Here's how 👇"

---

Each of these hooks targets different psychological triggers—pain relief, curiosity, validation, or aspiration—and invites readers to engage with your thread.
