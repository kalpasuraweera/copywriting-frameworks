# Cold Outreach Email: Freelance Copywriting Pitch

Here's a ready-to-send email, plus a few notes on customization below.

---

**Subject line options (pick one):**
- Congrats on the raise — quick question about your messaging
- [Company Name]'s next chapter needs words that convert
- Saw your seed round — one thing most founders overlook next

---

**Email:**

> Hi [Founder's Name],
>
> Congrats on closing your seed round — [Company Name] landing that is a strong signal the market sees what you're building.
>
> I imagine the next few months are about turning that capital into traction: new hires, product launches, maybe your first real push on marketing. One thing that tends to get rushed in this phase is copy — the words on your landing page, emails, and pitch materials that actually convert interest into customers (or your next round of investors).
>
> I'm a freelance copywriter who works with early-stage startups to sharpen that messaging — landing pages, email sequences, investor updates, product launch copy — so it sounds as sharp as the product you've built.
>
> A couple of recent examples of the kind of work I do: [1-line result, e.g., "Rewrote a SaaS homepage that lifted signup conversion by 22%"] and [another quick proof point].
>
> No pressure at all — happy to send over a quick teardown of your current landing page copy with 2-3 concrete suggestions, no strings attached. If it's useful, great; if not, no worries.
>
> Would love to help you make the most of this raise. Open to a 15-minute call this week or next?
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]
> [Portfolio link]
> [Phone/LinkedIn]

---

### A few tips to make this land:
1. **Personalize the opener** — mention the specific round size, lead investor, or something from their launch post (TechCrunch, LinkedIn announcement, etc.). Generic congrats emails get deleted fast.
2. **Lead with value, not ask** — the free teardown offer is doing the heavy lifting here; it's low-risk for them and shows your skill immediately.
3. **Keep it short** — founders post-raise are slammed. Under 150 words in the body is ideal.
4. **Proof over claims** — swap in real metrics/portfolio links if you have them; if you're newer, use a strong sample piece instead.
