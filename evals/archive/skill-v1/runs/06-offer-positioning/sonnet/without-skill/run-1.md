# Your Six P's (Marketing Mix for Service Businesses)

Here's the classic 6 P framework applied to your bookkeeping service:

## 1. Product
**What you're actually selling:** Not "bookkeeping" — you're selling *clean, decision-ready financials* so e-commerce founders know their true margins, cash position, and tax exposure without touching a spreadsheet.

- Core service: Monthly bookkeeping (categorization, reconciliation, financial statements)
- Likely add-ons: Inventory/COGS tracking, sales tax nexus monitoring, Amazon/Shopify/multi-channel reconciliation, profit dashboards
- Differentiator: Specialization in e-commerce (COGS, inventory, merchant fees, multi-platform reconciliation) vs. generic bookkeepers who fumble Shopify/Amazon settlement reports

## 2. Price
**$3,000/month** — position this deliberately:
- Too cheap to be "cheap outsourced labor," priced like a strategic hire
- Compare against cost of a part-time in-house bookkeeper ($4-6k/mo + benefits + management time) or a generalist firm that doesn't understand e-commerce (cheaper but costs them in bad decisions/missed tax issues)
- At $1-5M revenue, $36k/year is <1-3% of revenue — frame as insurance against bad inventory/margin decisions, not an expense

## 3. Place (Distribution/Access)
Where and how clients find and engage you:
- Direct outreach (LinkedIn, founder communities, Facebook groups for DTC/Amazon sellers)
- Referral partners: e-commerce CPAs, fractional CFOs, agency owners, Shopify Plus partners
- Content/SEO around e-commerce-specific bookkeeping pain (COGS methods, sales tax nexus, inventory accounting)
- Sales process: likely a discovery call → books review/audit → proposal

## 4. Promotion
The message and channels:
- Pain-first messaging: "Your books are lying to you" / "You don't actually know your margins"
- Channels: LinkedIn organic + ads, referral incentives, podcast guesting (e-commerce/DTC shows), case studies with real numbers
- Proof: before/after financials, time saved, tax penalties avoided, inventory shrinkage caught

## 5. People
- You/your team's credibility: e-commerce specialization, certifications (QBO ProAdvisor, Xero certified), specific platform expertise (A2X, Link My Books, etc.)
- Client-facing team consistency — founders want to know they're not getting rotated junior staff
- Ideal client profile: $1-5M e-commerce brands who've outgrown DIY books or a generalist bookkeeper and are making decisions off bad numbers

## 6. Process
- Onboarding: books cleanup/catch-up before monthly service starts
- Monthly cadence: reconciliation → categorization → financial statement delivery → optional review call
- Tech stack: QBO/Xero + A2X/Link My Books + inventory tools
- Turnaround time (e.g., books closed by the 15th of each month) — this is a real differentiator since late books are a huge pain point
