# The Single Biggest Fix

**Replace vague benefits with a specific problem and measurable outcome.**

Right now, this reads like a feature list, not a reason to care. "Collaborate seamlessly," "boost productivity," "unlock potential" — these are hollow marketing phrases that apply to every collaboration tool.

## What to do instead:

Pick ONE specific pain point your customers actually experience. For example:

- *If* the problem is context-switching overhead: "Stop losing 8 hours a week context-switching between chat, email, and spreadsheets. Teamory keeps everything in one place."
- *If* the problem is project delays: "Ship projects 30% faster with centralized task management and real-time visibility."
- *If* the problem is miscommunication: "Eliminate confusion. Every decision, file, and conversation lives in one searchable timeline."

Then show **concrete proof** ("Trusted by businesses of all sizes" means nothing — *name* them, or show a metric).

---

**Why this matters:** You're currently selling *features* to people who don't yet believe they have a problem. Start with the problem, then the solution becomes obvious.
