# Your CTA is doing zero work. Let me show you why—and fix it.

**What's wrong:**
- **Who**: No specific avatar ("interested" applies to anyone or no one)
- **What**: Vague action ("reach out" is passive and undefined)
- **When**: No deadline ("anytime" = no urgency, no priority)
- **How**: Not specific (email? form? phone? Slack?)

"Feel free" is also a soft landing—you're essentially saying "this is optional and requires effort from you." People rarely push back against that invitation.

---

## Strong CTA formula: **Who / What / When / How**

A strong CTA names the reader, states the exact action, gives a deadline or reason to move now, and makes the mechanism frictionless.

---

## Here are three rewrites (pick the one that matches your context):

**If this is an email or landing page asking them to book a call:**
> **"[Your name], book a 20-minute call with me by Friday at 2pm."** [clickable link]

*or, if you want to lead with benefit:*
> **"See if this applies to your situation. Book a 20-minute call here."** [link]

---

**If this is a cold outreach and you want a low-friction reply:**
> **"Reply with one thing that's been your biggest bottleneck this year. I'll send you a specific idea within 24 hours."**

*Why this works: you've named the avatar (them), the action (reply), the deadline (24h), and the payoff (specific idea, not vague advice).*

---

**If you want them to take a specific next step (download, purchase, apply):**
> **"Ready to [specific outcome]? [Action] now—takes 3 minutes."** [button]

---

1. **What's the actual next step?** (Book a call, buy, apply, download, reply, etc.)
2. **What's a real deadline?** (Do they lose a discount? Does an offer expire? Is there a cohort start date?)
3. **Where does this appear?** (Email, landing page, cold outreach, social post, ad?)
