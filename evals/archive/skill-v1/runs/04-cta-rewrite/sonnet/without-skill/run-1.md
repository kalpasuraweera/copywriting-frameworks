Here's what's killing your current CTA: it's passive ("feel free"), vague ("learning more"), and removes urgency ("anytime"). It reads like an invitation, not a call to action.

Here are stronger options depending on your goal:

**If you want a low-friction click:**
> "Get the details — takes 2 minutes."

**If you're selling a call/consult:**
> "Book your free 15-min call — spots fill fast."

**If it's a product/demo:**
> "See it in action. Start your free trial today."

**If you want urgency without being pushy:**
> "Ready to get started? Let's talk — this week's slots are limited."

**If you want curiosity-driven:**
> "Curious how this works for you? One click gets you the answer."

**Core principles I applied:**
1. Replaced passive phrasing ("feel free," "anytime") with a direct verb (Get, Book, See, Start).
2. Added a concrete next step instead of "learning more."
3. Introduced light urgency or specificity (time estimate, limited spots) — vague CTAs get ignored, specific ones get clicked.
4. Focused on *their* benefit, not your availability.
