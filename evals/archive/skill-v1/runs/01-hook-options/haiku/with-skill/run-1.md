# 5 Hook Options for Home-Cooking Meal-Kit Twitter Thread

**Quick assumption check:** I'm targeting parents who *want* to cook real meals but feel crushed on time/energy. The brand's edge is "shortcuts that don't feel like shortcuts" — real ingredients, simple preps, guilt-free. If that's wrong, let me know and I'll recalibrate.

---

## Hook Candidates (scored by strength)

**1. Problem-First / Pain-Based** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
> Your kids ask "what's for dinner" and your brain goes blank. Again.

**Why it works:** High relevance (happens daily), high perceived effort to fix (planning from scratch is brutal), credibility through relatability. Doesn't mention the product — just hooks the feeling.

---

**2. Outcome / Benefit-Forward** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
> Home-cooked dinner on the table in 30 minutes. No planning, no 7-ingredient shopping runs, no guilt.

**Why it works:** Specific outcome (30 min is testable), stacks three benefits (speed + simplicity + relief), speaks directly to the three-part pain of busy parents.

---

**3. Identity / Value-Tension** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
> You don't want to be the parent ordering takeout 4x a week. Here's how you actually don't.

**Why it works:** Hooks on internal guilt/aspiration (parents care deeply about this), feels like a solution, not a lecture. High emotional leverage.

---

**4. Contrarian / Objection-Flip** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
> Meal kits are expensive and wasteful. Ours aren't.

**Why it works:** Addresses the most common objection upfront, gives permission to people already skeptical. Better for converting fence-sitters than cold traffic.

---

**5. Data / Proof-Based** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **(if the number is real)**
> We've saved busy parents 14 hours a month on meal planning and cooking.

**Why it works:** Concrete, quantified benefit (time saved = currency for busy people), suggests testing behind it. *Only use if you have real data.*

---

**My pick:** **#1 or #4** as your opener — one hooks the pain, one gives permission. Then the thread can deliver the solution (outcome). Which resonates more with your audience's top objection?
