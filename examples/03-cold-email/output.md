### The thinking

- **People:** a technical or product-minded founder who closed a seed round within the last 30 days. They're about to hire and spend on growth. They're getting flooded with vendor emails and have no time.
- **Positioning:** most freelancers pitch "I write great copy." You show up having already done the work on *their* site.
- **Promise:** the seed money they're about to spend on ads and sales lands on a site that actually converts. The core outcome: runway goes further, and the next investor update shows traction instead of excuses.
- **Proof:** the audit in the email *is* the proof. After that, add one real result from past work. `[placeholder, don't invent it]`
- **Priority:** real cost of waiting. The money goes into ads and hires within months, and it all points at a homepage written before they had customers.
- **Process:** this uses the TAG approach. **T**arget one founder, **A**udit their site for real, then **G**ift them a rewrite before asking for anything.

**Before you send:** points 1–3 below only work if they're genuinely about *their* site. Spend 30–60 minutes on the audit. If you only find two real issues, send two. Don't pad the list.

### Subject line options

| Subject | Benefit | Relevance | Credibility | Effort elsewhere |
|---------|---------|-----------|-------------|------------------|
| **"[Company]'s homepage, rewritten (free)"** | High | Very high | High (it's already done) | High |
| "Congrats on the round — quick idea" | Low | Medium | Low (every vendor sends this) | Low |
| "3 fixes before the seed money hits ads" | High | High | Medium | Medium |

Going with the first. It's specific to them, and it tells them the value is already sitting in the email.

---

**Subject:** [Company]'s homepage, rewritten (free)

Hi [First name],

Congrats on the seed round. Saw the news on [source].

Here's the part nobody warns you about: over the next few months, most of that money goes into ads and sales hires. All of it points at the homepage you wrote before you had customers.

I spent an hour on [company.com]. Three things stood out:

1. The headline, "[current headline]", describes what the product *is*. A [their buyer] skimming it can't tell what changes for them.
2. [Specific gap, e.g. "No proof anywhere from a customer like the mid-market teams you're now targeting."]
3. [Specific gap, e.g. "The only CTA asks for a 30-minute demo before anyone has seen the product."]

So I rewrote your hero section. It's a one-page doc: [link]. It's yours to use, whether we ever work together or not.

I'm a freelance copywriter for [niche] startups. [One real proof line, e.g. "I rewrote [client]'s onboarding emails, and [real result]."]

If the rewrite's useful, reply "more" and I'll do your pricing page next, this week.

[Your name]

---

**CTA check:** *Who:* this founder. *What:* reply "more". *When:* this week. *How:* a one-word reply. One ask. It's easy to say yes to, and the risk is on you, not them.
