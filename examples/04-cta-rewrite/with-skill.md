**Rewrite:**

"Schedule your free strategy call this week. [Schedule now →]"

**Why it works better:**
- **Specific action** ("schedule" vs. "reach out") — reader knows exactly what to do
- **Clear benefit** ("strategy call" vs. "learning more") — they know what they're getting
- **Natural timeframe** ("this week" vs. "anytime") — bounded time triggers action instead of killing it
- **Direct mechanism** (link or button) — no friction

---

**For other offer types:**
- *Product/software trial:* "Start your free 7-day trial today — no card needed. [Get started →]"
- *Content/resource:* "Get instant access — drop your email below. [Unlock →]"

---

The core problem with your original: "Feel free to reach out anytime" does the exact opposite of what a CTA should do. It removes urgency, makes the reader guess what to do, and puts all the work on them. A strong CTA is **specific** (an action), **bounded** (a timeframe), and **frictionless** (clear mechanism).
