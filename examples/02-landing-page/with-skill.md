# Hero Section — 6-Week Beginner Marathon Plan

**HEADLINE**  
Train for Your First Marathon in 6 Weeks

**SUBHEAD**  
Finish strong and uninjured — without 16 weeks of training.

**BODY**  
Most beginner plans ask for 16–20 weeks. By week 8, you're burned out.

This isn't one of them.

The 6-Week Beginner Marathon Plan cuts straight to the essential work: building the endurance, mental toughness, and race-day clarity to finish 26.2 miles. Every workout is purposeful. Every week builds toward race day. No filler. No guessing.

You already know you can run. What you need now is a plan sharp enough to work and short enough to actually stick with.

**CTA**  
Start the 6-Week Plan

---

**Notes:**  
The positioning leans on speed (6 vs. 16 weeks) and specificity (not a generic plan) to address the real pain point: beginner runners get overwhelmed by long plans and quit before race day. The copy assumes your audience is someone who's already committed to running a marathon, has *some* running base, and is looking for a plan now because race day is near. If your audience is completely new to running, or if the plan requires a specific aerobic baseline to work safely, that's worth clarifying in the body or a secondary section.
